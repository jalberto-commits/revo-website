Do I have to change my business phone number?

No. You keep your number. Revo picks up the calls you'd otherwise miss, or all of them if you'd rather never touch the phone.

How does it know what to say about my business?

You tell it once: services, service area, hours, pricing rules, what counts as an emergency. Revo follows that on every call and never goes off script.

Can I read or listen to what was said?

Yes. Every call is transcribed and summarized in the app, with the caller's name, number, job, and address. You can review it from your phone or desktop.

What if a caller needs to talk to a real person?

Revo can transfer the call to you or a team member based on the rules you set, for example, emergencies during business hours.

Does it work with my calendar or CRM?

Revo connects with your existing calendar, CRM, and booking tools so appointments and call data land where you already work.

Is my customer data secure?

Yes. Revo uses bank-level encryption and strict data protection protocols. Your business and customer information stays confidential.
